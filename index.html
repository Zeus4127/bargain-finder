Questions

Good to know

Where do the prices come from?

Real sold prices and auction results built into the app, the sold prices you add (eBay Terapeak is free for sellers), and your own sales. Asking prices count for much less than sales.

Does it work for caravans, trailers and boats?

Yes, along with cars, utes, motorbikes, mowers and tools. Towing costs and the right certificates are counted for each type.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is worked out on your device. The only thing that goes online is the optional deal alert, and only if you switch it on.

Is it really free?

Yes: no ads, no sign-up and no paid version.

How do I install the Android app?

Tap "Download for Android" on your phone, open the file, and allow installing from your browser when Android asks. It isn't on the Play Store.
