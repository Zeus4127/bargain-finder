Questions

Good to know.

Where do the prices come from?

1,628 real auction results built into the app, the sold prices you add (eBay's Terapeak research is free for sellers), and your own sales. Asking prices count for much less than sales, because sellers ask high.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and no paid version.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is worked out on your device, even the demo on this page. The only thing that ever goes online is the optional deal alert, and only if you switch it on.

What can it check?

Cars from Corollas to Porsches, utes and 4WDs, side-by-sides like the Polaris RZR, dirt bikes, jet skis, boats, caravans and trailers, luxury watches like Rolex and Omega, mowers and tools. For collectables like trading cards, Flip Pro values them from the sold prices you add.

How do I get the Android app?

Tap "Get the Android app" on your phone, open the file, and allow installing from your browser when Android asks. It isn't on the Play Store, and it's the same app as this website, plus sharing from Facebook and deal alerts.
