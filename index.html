Questions

Good to know.

Where do the prices come from?

1,628 real auction results built into the app, the sold prices you add (eBay's Terapeak research is free for sellers), and your own sales. Asking prices count for much less than sales, because sellers ask high.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and no paid version.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is worked out on your device, even the demo on this page. The only thing that ever goes online is the optional deal alert, and only if you switch it on.

What can it check?

Cars from Corollas to Porsches, utes and 4WDs, side-by-sides like the Polaris RZR, dirt bikes, jet skis, boats, caravans and trailers, luxury watches like Rolex and Omega, mowers and tools. For collectables like trading cards, Flip Pro values them from the sold prices you add.

How do I get the Android app?

Tap "Get the Android app" on your phone, open the file, and allow installing from your browser when Android asks. It isn't on the Play Store, and it's the same app as this website, plus sharing from Facebook and deal alerts.

For experts

Under the hood.

Exactly how Bargain Finder works out a value, what it counts, and where it's weak. Nothing hidden.

How a value is worked out

1. The ad is read: make, model, trim, year, kilometres, place, and warning words. 2. A new price comes from the built-in price guide for that model and trim. 3. An age and kilometre curve gives what a typical one keeps (for example about 55% at 10 years for a Hilux). 4. Brand strength nudges it (Toyota holds value better). 5. Real auction results for the same model check the answer. 6. The fair price, an opening offer (worked out from the asking price and the fair price) and a walk-away price follow, with a confidence level: fairly sure, somewhat sure, or rough guess.

The 11 red-flag checks

Statutory or repairable write-off, no papers, stolen check reminder, finance owing, scam patterns (deposit first, shipping only, seller away), odometer too low for the age, rust, needs work or not running, no rego or no safety certificate, flood damage, hail damage. Plus "too good to be true" when the price is under 40% of value, and a PPSR reminder for every vehicle.

How it keeps itself honest

Every deal it flags is saved once with only what was known that day. When it sells (or a similar one does), the real profit % after costs is scored against "flag nothing". Older deals (70%) tune the rule; newer deals (30%) get one look; the luck bar rises with the number of rules tried (z of at least 2.87 for 24 rules). Under 30 followed-up deals it says "too few to tell". Values are only corrected after the correction proves itself on newer deals.

Limits

Trading cards and some caravans can't be valued from the ad alone: it says "need more info", and Flip Pro values them from sold prices you add. If a bike, quad or buggy ad leaves out the year, it guesses a typical age, says so, and marks the price as low confidence. Values assume good order; condition, options and modifications change them. Costs are set for Queensland. It isn't financial advice: always inspect in person and do a PPSR check.
