Add eBay sold results (free)

How to get them from eBay Terapeak (5 steps)
  1. On a computer, sign in to ebay.com.au and open Seller Hub (My eBay → Selling → Seller Hub; it's free to turn on).
  2. Go to Research (Terapeak product research). It's free for sellers.
  3. Type what you're checking, e.g. Hilux SR 2014, set Sold and Last 90 days (or 1 year).
  4. Select the results list (drag over it) and copy, or use Download if it's offered.
  5. Paste it in the box below and tap Add sold prices. Email the text to yourself to paste it on the phone.

A CSV with a title column and a price column works too (date, year and km columns are used if they're there).
