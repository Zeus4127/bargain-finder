    How much of today's new price a van keeps

    A van loses about 12% in its first year, then roughly 2 to 3% of the new price each year (checked against real Queensland listings in November 2026). Premium brands like Bushtracker and Kedron lose value slowest, and budget brands lose it fastest. Hail or dent damage takes a further 15 to 30% off, which is why it suits a van that stays parked.
