Privacy

Bargain Finder app and website. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version: Bargain Finder has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share anything about you. What you save stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Ads, screenshots and alert emails you share or paste in.
  • Your saved items, history, inspection checklists, sale prices you record, and backups.
  • Reading text from screenshots and the photo recogniser both run on your phone. Your pictures are not uploaded.

On the website, the same things are kept in your browser's own storage on your device. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.

Deal alerts (optional, off until you turn them on)

If you turn on deal alerts in More > Deal alerts, the app checks the free ntfy.sh service about once an hour, using the alert name you type in (for example https://ntfy.sh/your-alert-name/json). It only reads new alert messages for that name. Nothing about you or your saved items is sent. ntfy.sh, like any web server, can see the internet address the check comes from; see the ntfy.sh privacy policy.

Turn alerts off at any time in the same place.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, log-in or account.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Pictures come in only when you choose to share or pick them.
  • Bargain Finder does not log in to or copy data from Facebook, Gumtree, CaravanCampingSales or any other website. It works only with what you give it.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as internet addresses) to run and protect its service; see the GitHub privacy statement. Bargain Finder itself doesn't receive or keep them.

Children

Bargain Finder is made for adults buying and selling second-hand goods. It is not aimed at children.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, delete items in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear the website's data in your browser).

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
